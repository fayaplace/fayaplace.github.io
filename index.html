Good to Know

One of the things that makes Davao special is how seriously the city takes clean, smoke-free public spaces. Davao has enforced smoke-free policies for many years, helping protect residents, visitors, children and workers from secondhand smoke — and keeping shared spaces healthier and more comfortable for everyone.

Please observe Davao City's no-smoking rules throughout the property and in designated public areas.
