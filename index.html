Good to know

Smoke-Free Davao

Davao City is known nationwide for the strict implementation of its smoke-free ordinance. Smoking is prohibited in public places, including malls, restaurants, transport terminals, and common areas of residential buildings.

This is part of what keeps the city's air clean and its public spaces comfortable for everyone. We appreciate your understanding and cooperation during your stay.
